What you are watching

A trained artificial network follows a measured fruit-fly visual-to-movement circuit. Each of its 1,230 units represents a selected FlyWire neuron. Its 8,515 directed connections follow the recorded wiring.

Our visual encoder and paddle decoder are engineering choices. Training used supervised teaching followed by reinforcement learning. The browser now runs the saved calculations; it is not learning during play.
